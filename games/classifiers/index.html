How to read IPA and tone marks

Mid ˧ · Low ˨˩ · Falling ˥˩ · High ˦˥ · Rising ˩˩˦. Tone marks follow each syllable. ː = long vowel; ʰ = aspiration; ʔ = glottal stop; . = syllable boundary.

Words, letter names and practice syllables carry tones. Individual consonant and vowel sounds do not have an inherent tone.
